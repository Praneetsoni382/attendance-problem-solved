# AttendWise visual guidelines

These principles come from the supplied AttendWise product brief and its
multi-screen UI reference. `tokens.json` is the single source of truth for exact
color, type, radius, and spacing values.

## Product feel

AttendWise should feel professional and trustworthy without becoming a rigid
college ERP portal. Keep it friendly to students, straightforward for
non-technical users, and polished without visual clutter.

## Color and status

- Use the deep indigo primary for the most important action and active state.
- Use violet as a supporting brand color, not as a competing primary action.
- Use mint for positive attendance and completion states.
- Use the red destructive role for absence/error states and amber from the
  chart palette for potential discrepancies.
- Pair each status color with a clear text label or icon.
- Keep the cool off-white page background, white surfaces, and dark navy text
  in light mode; preserve the same hierarchy in dark mode.

## Surfaces and hierarchy

- Use rounded cards with restrained shadows and subtle borders.
- Keep a consistent spacing rhythm and clear distinction between page headings,
  supporting labels, and dense attendance data.
- Use progress indicators for percentages, but keep the numeric value visible.
- Use decorative academic illustrations only when they support the task.

## Responsive behavior and motion

- On mobile, reflow navigation, timetable, and history content for touch and
  readability instead of shrinking desktop layouts.
- Use subtle transitions and honor reduced-motion preferences.
- Maintain visible keyboard focus and readable contrast in both themes.

## Product language

- Keep labels direct and understandable to students.
- Describe ERP differences as potential discrepancies, not proof that a record
  is wrong.
- Describe attendance targets as personal targets, not institutional policy.

## Brand assets

The retained image is a multi-screen app UI reference, not a standalone logo.
